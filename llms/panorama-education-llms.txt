# Panorama Education

> Panorama Education is a K-12 education technology company providing a student
> success platform used by school districts to turn student data into action —
> academic and behavior (MTSS/RTI), universal screening, social-emotional
> learning (SEL) and survey instruments, attendance, graduation planning, and
> family/staff engagement. Panorama has no public self-service developer API;
> integrations are delivered as nightly B2B data sync with student information
> systems (via the OneRoster standard and SFTP), not a documented REST API.

## Integration Standards
- OneRoster (data import standard for roster/SIS data)
- Nightly synchronization with 24+ Student Information Systems (PowerSchool, Infinite Campus, Skyward, Aspen, Synergy, Tyler SIS, and others)
- Secure File Transfer (SFTP) for district data files
